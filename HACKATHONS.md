# Devpost shortlist checked October 27, 2026

User requirements: free entry, cash prizes, online, preferably solo; India resident, adult, student; 16 GB RAM. Cash prizes are competitive awards, not guaranteed participant pay. Source pages can change; recheck before registering.

## Recommended now: PayPal AI Hackathon

[Overview](https://paypalaihackathon.devpost.com/) · [Official rules](https://paypalaihackathon.devpost.com/rules)

Online; individual entries allowed; no purchase required. The overview advertises $67,500+ in prizes, including a $12,000 first prize. Adult entrants are allowed; India is not among the listed excluded jurisdictions, subject to the other eligibility/conflict restrictions. You still need an eligible developer account.

The rules specify November 12, 2026 at noon Pacific: **November 13 at 1:30 AM IST**. Another page showed 2 PM Pacific; use the earlier rules deadline and target November 11 for your own final check.

Requires a functioning PayPal sandbox integration plus meaningful AI, public source code with an open-source license, runnable instructions or a hosted demo, English materials, and a public YouTube demonstration under three minutes. This is the best currently open fit for LedgerLens, but a synthetic pipeline alone does not qualify.

## More learning time: Kiroween 2026

[Overview](https://kiroween.devpost.com/) · [Rules notice](https://kiroween.devpost.com/rules)

Online; solo entry explicitly allowed; advertised $100,000 cash pool. Announced window: October 30–December 4, 2026. The overview states December 4 at 2 PM Pacific, equivalent to **December 5 at 3:30 AM IST**.

**Do not begin a Kiroween project before submissions open.** The page explicitly restricts eligibility to new projects made during the submission period. This LedgerLens prototype was created October 27 and should not be presented as a newly eligible Kiroween project. Full rules and submission details are not yet published, so country eligibility and all costs/conditions cannot yet be confirmed. A valid Kiro user ID or AWS account ID is needed for registration. Reassess on October 30; do not treat this as fully verified today.

## Technically possible, too rushed: Qloo Agentic Hackathon

[Overview](https://qloo.devpost.com/) · [Official rules](https://qloo.devpost.com/rules)

Online; individual entrants allowed; no purchase necessary; $25,000 cash pool. Adult eligibility with country exclusions applies; India is not on the listed exclusions. Deadline **October 31 at 9:15 AM IST** (October 30, 11:45 PM EDT).

Requires the Qloo API and an agentic application, public licensed source, and an externally hosted functional demo. A recommendation-data pipeline would fit better than payment quality. The short remaining time and API access/hosting work make this a weak first choice.

## Student fallback with prize ambiguity: Arbiter Hacks V1

[Overview](https://arbiter-hacks-v1.devpost.com/) · [Rules](https://arbiter-hacks-v1.devpost.com/rules)

Online; students aged 13+; solo teams allowed. Deadline **December 21 at 10:30 AM IST** (December 20, 9 PM PST). General track accepts any working project. Public source, a 2–3 minute demo, and a written description are required.

The advertised $7,800 headline mixes cash-labelled amounts with credits. The detailed descriptions say $100 cash for first place in each track and $50 for second; the rules still list prizes as TBD. Do not interpret the headline as verified cash. Entry-fee terms and prize details need organizer confirmation before treating this as a fully qualified fallback.

## Decision

Use PayPal if you can obtain sandbox reporting access and an AI API account. If those become blockers, choose a different currently eligible open-ended event rather than pretending synthetic data satisfies sponsor integration. Kiroween offers a longer window for a **different, new project**, once its rules are available.
